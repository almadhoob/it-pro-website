Initiative Overview

The Foundation of Our Community

The IT Pro Community is a virtual initiative in the Kingdom of Bahrain designed to help technology professionals survive and evolve in the era of Large Language Models. Bringing together computer scientists, software engineers, data engineers, solution architects, system administrators, cybersecurity specialists, and User Experience and User Interface (UX/UI) designers, the community champions the craftsmanship of AI-assisted software engineering over superficial "vibe coding" and AI-generated bloatware, welcoming both seasoned practitioners and dedicated learners eager to master rigorous engineering from experts. The initiative also hosts a dedicated Linux User Group to promote free culture and open collaboration. While actively supporting Bahrainization and the advancement of national talent within the local IT sector, the community remains inclusive and open to expatriate professionals of all nationalities. Operating primarily through focused WhatsApp groups with plans for future in-person gatherings, the initiative can be reached via its official website at www.it-bh.pro and via email at info@it-bh.pro. The community was founded in October 2026 by Ameen Altajer, Ahmed Almadhoob, and a collective of Bahraini developers.

Our Philosophy

Craftsmanship Over Vibe Coding

We stand firmly for engineering discipline, algorithmic competence, and verifiable system design in an age flooded with unverified generative code.

What We Champion

AI-Assisted Engineering

  • AI amplifies engineering rigor, never replaces it.
  • Algorithmic efficiency, clean architecture, and maintainable codebases.
  • High-efficiency, secure, and reliable production systems.
  • Earnest learners who respect software engineering discipline and want to learn from seasoned experts.
What We Reject

Vibe-Coded Bloatware

  • Uncritical generation of fragile, inefficient bloatware.
  • Bypassing fundamental computer science and engineering principles.
  • Blindly prompting LLMs without understanding architecture, security, or debugging.

Dedicated Spotlight: Linux User Group

Championing Free Culture and Open Collaboration

Alongside its focus on AI-assisted engineering, the community is home to a dedicated Linux User Group (LUG). Built on the principles of free culture and open collaboration, this group provides a collaborative space for open-source enthusiasts, systems engineers, and developers to share knowledge, advocate for software freedom, and explore the open technologies that power modern computing infrastructure.

National Leadership & Global Inclusivity

Supporting Bahrainization, Welcoming Global Talent

At its core, The IT Pro Community is deeply committed to supporting Bahrainization within the local information technology sector. By fostering high-level mentorship, peer code reviews, and architectural discussions, we aim to cultivate world-class Bahraini technical leadership capable of driving the Kingdom's digital economy forward. At the same time, we recognize that great engineering thrives on diverse perspectives; our doors are wide open to expatriate IT professionals of all nationalities who contribute to Bahrain's vibrant technology ecosystem.
